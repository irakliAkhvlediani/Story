Our Story

Where Every Meal
Tells a Story

Nestled in the heart of Kutaisi, Story is more than a restaurant — it's a journey through Georgia's rich culinary heritage, reimagined with contemporary flair. Our kitchen honors centuries-old traditions while embracing modern techniques and global influences.

From the smoky aroma of our mtsvadi to the delicate layers of our signature cocktails, every detail at Story is crafted to create moments worth remembering. Warm ambiance, attentive service, and flavors that linger long after the last bite.
